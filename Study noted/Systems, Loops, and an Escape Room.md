We say "systems" a lot. Combat, crafting, stealth, physics... Can we have a system system?

We're built for stories and huge systemic issues are difficult to understand in their entierty. Systemic harms and effects stay invisible until something breaks (see [[Cardboard Ghosts]], chapter 1). Unless you can play with them -> see how different elements interact.

Systems are how the nouns (properties) are used with the verbs in the game. Objects, properties and behaviours, and relationships.

A checker has color, location, type (normal/king). RPG characters have loads of stats and behaviours.

Games can have perfect information or imperfect information.

# Advanced Wars (an example)
Units have different costs, range, vision, ammo, and behaviours.

The environment have cost, changes in movement speed. They interact with units with those.

Combat is an interaction between multiple objects. It is a calculation between the weapon type of attacker/defender, their HP, and the environment, as well as a some element of chance. Probability is random but designed. Decisisions are based on incomplete information.

# Game economics
Sources things come from. Holds for them until needed. A converter makes one resource into another. Desciders determine if you can or can't do something because of a resource or lack of it. Drains use up the resource for nothing.

## Complicated vs Complex
Complicated when they have many parts but they are very fixed. Complex ones make resources be used in many ways.

* Reinforcing loops ("positive feedback")
	* A increases B with increases A, etc. Isn't good, usually.
* Balancing loops ("negative feedback")
	* Strives towards equilibrium.
	* Can feel like rubber banding
