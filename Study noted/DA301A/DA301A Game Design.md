#teach/Raquel-Robinson
First half of autumn 2026. Basic game design skill. 

Raquel used to work on affective games. Physiological signals as controllers.

### Learning goals
- show understanding of basic concepts in game analysis and game design
- show understanding of the importance of usability and accessibility in game development
- show basic knowledge of gaming experiences
- show the ability to analyze games for design reasons as a basis
- show the ability to create, develop and implement a prototype for game design
- reflect on the relationship between design choice and the user's experiences
