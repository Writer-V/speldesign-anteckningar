# Reflecting on games
We're required to playtest... even if there's no time to do so?

Raquel isn't a fan of how prescriptive #Game-Design-Workshop and #Rules-of-Play are. There are meaningul things in them, but they should be taken with a grain of salt.

Salen & Zimmerman: *A game is a system in hich players engage in artificial conflict, that results in a quantifiable outcome.*

Every single part of that can, and probably should, be questioned. Basically: it's a game if people engage in it as a game.
