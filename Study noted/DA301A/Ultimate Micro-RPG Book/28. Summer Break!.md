# Summer Break!

Defend Your Childhood

By Joey Barranco

![[umrpg-f0075-01.jpg]]

**NUMBER OF PLAYERS:** 1–4

**PLAYING TIME:** 30–60 minutes

**COMPLEXITY:** 3

**YOU’LL NEED:** 1d6, paper, pen.

**GOAL:** Build a childhood Fort and defend it from the threats of adulthood.

**TAGS:** Genre: Modern, Tone: Adventurous, Format: GMless, Content: Monsters, nostalgia, growing up

Before getting thrown face-first into the ferocious trauma that is high school, cling to your final summer break with friends and defend your childhood Fort from dangers that definitely are not allegories for adulthood!

## Setup

Every kid is good at something. Distribute 6 points between **Think**, **Feel**, and **Do** Talents to determine the modifier added to each dice roll (1d6 + Talent).

- **Think:** Any action that requires a kid to use their brain—calculating, hacking, tinkering
- **Feel:** Any action that requires a kid to use their heart—motivating, deceiving, communicating
- **Do:** Any action that requires a kid to use their body—jumping, running, throwing

Now, give your kid a name, pick an age (8–14), and determine your kid’s appearance and attitude.

Take a moment to discuss how each of your kids knows each other. Do they attend the same school? Play sports together? Are they related?

Once you’ve built your kids, it’s time to build your Fort!

### Build Your Fort

Your Fort is sacred. It is a place of safety, fun, and independence. It is a place where grownups can’t enter. Build your Fort by spending Fort Points (FP). FP is determined by the number of kids + 6. Add up the amount of FP from each Fort trait to determine your Fort’s Hit Points (HP) for the game.

- Fortified Walls (cost 2 FP): Strong walls: give +2 HP.
- Elevated/Underground Fort (cost 3 FP): Your Fort is in a tree or in a cave: add +3 HP.
- Weapons (cost 5 FP): Your Fort has weapons such as water balloons, slingshots, or snowballs. Your kid can make a direct attack against a Danger with a +2 to your action roll: add +1 HP.
- Alarm System (cost 1 FP): Once per game, the kids can act first on a turn: add +1 HP.
- Electricity (cost 2 FP): Your Fort has electricity: add +2 HP.

### Select Your Danger

Danger manifests from the fears of growing up. Roll 1d6 to determine the Danger of your game. Each Danger has a unique style and abilities.

Each Danger makes an attack against your Fort by rolling 1d6 + number of kids. If the Danger rolls higher than your kid, it damages your Fort by 2 HP. If the Danger rolls a natural 6, it automatically triggers its special ability listed in the following table. Each Danger has 6 + number of kids’ worth of HP.

1. 1. **THE ALIEN:** Makes a second attack.
2. 2. **THE BEAST:** Can dig underground or climb a tree: Deals +2 damage.
3. 3. **THE CLOWN:** Causes fear: Each kid must roll higher than 5 with 1d6 + heart. The Fort loses 1 HP per failed roll.
4. 4. **THE DRAGON:** Flame breath deals +3 damage.
5. 5. **THE ROBOT:** EMP blast disables electricity and inflicts +1 damage.
6. 6. **THE SHADOW:** Contesting kid succumbs to the crushing sorrow of adulthood and takes a -2 penalty to all dice rolls for the remainder of the game.

## Gameplay

Your Danger will stop at nothing to destroy your Fort. You must unite with your friends and defend it!

- Take turns determining the actions and dice rolls for your Danger.
- Each player rolls a 1d6 to determine turn order.
- Each turn requires 1 Danger action and 1 kid action as contested rolls. The highest roll wins that turn, and the winner may cause 2 damage to the Danger or Fort, or repair 1 HP to the Fort.
- Danger attacks first followed by a kid. (Kids may go first once per game if an alarm system is installed in the Fort.)

### Sample Turn

The Robot (Danger) approaches the treehouse Fort, seeing Miguel (kid) on the balcony. It charges up a laser beam and fires at Miguel. Miguel ducks quickly and uses the Fort’s weapons system to dump a bucket of water onto The Robot. The Robot rolls a 1d6 + 4 for a total of 5, while Miguel, using a Think action for the water bucket, rolls a 1d6 + Think for a total of 6. Since Miguel rolled higher than The Robot, he wins the turn and decides that the water damages The Robot’s circuitry for 2 points.

## Ending the Game

The game ends if the Fort or the Danger is destroyed by reducing either HP to 0. If the Danger is destroyed, you hold onto your innocence for one more summer. If your Fort is destroyed, you must return home and leave your childhood behind.
