THE FINAL BATTLE IS HERE

# To the Bitter End
**NUMBER OF PLAYERS:** 2 • **PLAYING TIME:** 1–2 hours • **COMPLEXITY:** 2 • **YOU’LL NEED:** Pen and paper, 1d6. • **GOAL:** Win the Final Battle and decide the fate of your rival. • **TAGS:** Genre: Modern; Tone: Serious; Format: GMless; Content: Competition, rivalry, tragedy **BY TAKUMA OKADA**

*To the Bitter End* is a game about a pair of rivals locked in an epic conflict. In this game they’ll clash many times, over any setting that you can imagine: duels, cooking competitions, sports, racing, etc.

## SETUP
First, you’ll need the setting and tone of your game. Come up with a setting on your own, or choose something from the list of examples. Because this game can get dark, you should set the tone together before starting. A game of high school rivals might be more lighthearted than a game of two duelists with a deep grudge.

- A bounty hunter and their target, a space pirate, who has led them on a merry chase across half the galaxy.
- The top chef at a prestigious cooking academy and a transfer student who is rapidly climbing the ranks.
- A master of the blade, hunting down the vampire who killed their mentor.
- Two disciples of the greatest mage in the land.

Next, you’ll need your rivals. To create your character, come up with a name, what your character looks like, and answers to the following core questions. Write all of your answers down.

- What is a place that’s important to you?
- What is your dearest possession?
- Whose respect must you earn?
- Who can you trust?

These answers are your character’s Cores. The pain of losing them is what intensifies your rivalry.

Finally, your character needs two abilities. Your abilities can be anything, but they should be tied to your setting. Come up with them on your own, or choose from the list of examples. You’ll gain more abilities after each Rival Battle.

**ABILITIES**

- Sword fighting
- Acrobatics
- Nimble fingers
- Cooking
- Super strength
- Money
- Photographic memory
- A keen sense of smell
- X-ray vision
- Healing
- Perfect pitch
- Mathematics
- Invisibility
- Juggling
- Punching
- Singing
- Empathy
- Talking to animals

## GAMEPLAY
Once you’ve finished characters, you’re ready to play. *To the Bitter End* takes place over three Rival Battles and one Final Battle.

### RIVAL BATTLE
Go through these steps together. If you’re having trouble coming up with an idea, discuss it with each other. It’s only your characters who are at each other’s throats. Collaborate out of character and lean into dramatic irony.

1. **1.** Choose one of your rival’s Cores. Cross it out.
2. **2.** Roll the d6. **1** – **2** : Hurt or damage the Core. **3** – **4** : Steal or usurp the Core. **5** – **6** : Destroy or remove the Core.
3. **3.** Decide which ability or abilities you used to accomplish the result of your roll.
4. **4.** Meet on the battlefield. How have you arrived here?
5. **5.** Take turns taking actions. Each player should go two or three times per Rival Battle. Come up with actions on your own, or choose from the list of examples.

**ACTIONS**

- Use an ability to corner your rival.
- Reveal to your rival how you’ve taken out one of their Cores.
- Exploit a weakness in your rival’s ability with your own.
- Clash head-to-head, close enough to whisper something to your rival.
- Use an ability to beat your rival at something they’re good at.
- Land a hit with an ability. It won’t take them out, but it’ll hurt.
- Use an ability to trick your rival, giving you an advantage.
- When the situation looks dire, turn the tables on your rival with one of your abilities.

1. **6.** Part ways. What mark of this battle will your character remember when they think of their rival? Write it down. Then, your character gains an ability because of this mark.
2. **7.** Out of character, talk about how the game is going, what you liked and didn’t like. If you would like to remove or revise anything, talk about it now. Take a break if you need one. Then, start the next Rival Battle.

## ENDING THE GAME
When your rivals have only one Core left each, it’s time for the Final Battle.

Decide together why this final battlefield is significant. What is your history with this place?

Take actions like in step 5 of a Rival Battle. Come up with your own or choose from the Actions list.

When it feels like the battle is at its peak, play rock paper scissors. The winner describes how they defeat their rival. Which ability or combination of abilities gives them the edge they need?

The winner decides the fate of their rival.

Do they cross out their last Core? Do they spare them? Do they find some deep connection between them?

What does the winner do with their remaining Core?

Was it worth all this?
