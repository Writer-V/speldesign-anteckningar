# ABOUT THE AUTHORS
**James D’Amato** is the author of *The Ultimate RPG Character Backstory Guide* and *The Ultimate RPG Gameplay Guide*, as well as the creator and game master of *One Shot* podcast and *Campaign: Skyjacks* podcast dedicated to RPG gameplay. He trained at Second City and iO in Chicago in the art of improvisational comedy; he now uses that education to introduce new people to role-playing and incorporates improvisational storytelling techniques to create compelling and entertaining stories for RPG campaigns and one-shot adventures.

A game designer, writer, artist, and sneaker enthusiast born and raised in New Orleans and currently living in Durham, NC, **Omari Akil** has designed and published games independently since 2018 and seeks to continue pushing the limits of whom tabletop games can reach and the type of content they bring to the forefront. His premier board game, *Rap Godz*, connected hip-hop culture to board games in a way that has never been done before. He continues to explore game designs and themes that are linked to African diaspora and Afrofuturism in his quest to become a full-time gaming professional.

**Amr Ammourazz** is a writer, game designer, and podcaster who creates fiction to reflect Egyptian culture and the way it permeates throughout their perspective. Their games are designed to showcase how mechanics and narrative can work together to promote the same experience, while spanning the scope from emotionally intense to comic book–zany. By day, they yell at robots to move (a highly technical approach) and stare at numbers until they arrange themselves logically on a page. Sometimes, it even works!

**Anna Anthropy** is a game designer, educator, and thirtysomething teen witch. She is the author of seven books and too many games (many of which can be found at [w.itch.io](http://w.itch.io)), and she teaches game design as DePaul University’s Game Designer in Residence. She lives in Chicago with her familiar, a little black cat named Encyclopedia Frown.

**Joey “Gnomedic” Barranco**, of Chicago, is a game designer, broadcaster, and social media wizard. Recent collaborative storytelling work includes podcasts like *Rise of the Demigods* and streaming TTRPGs on Twitch.

**Caitlynn Aura Belle** (they/she/it) is a game designer, artist, and burlesque performer from Savannah, GA. Their game *A Real Game* won the Indie Groundbreaker Award for Game of the Year, and many of her works have appeared in podcasts and live streams. It has worked on many indie games during their *Kickstarter* runs, and also cowrote *Singularity: A Transhumanist Dating LARP*, with Ginger Goat. It can be found on *Twitter* at [@auracait](http://twitter.com/auracait).

**Sharang Biswas** is a writer, interactive theater maker, game designer, and artist. He has published games through Pelgrane Press, Cryptozoic Entertainment, *Rolled & Told* magazine, and *sub-Q* magazine, and created large-scale games for clients such as the Ford Foundation and the Museum of the Moving Image. He won the Dark Horse Award at the International Festival of Independent Games (IndieCade), and twice won the Indie Game Developer Network (IGDN) Most Innovative Game award. Sharang has taught and lectured on game design and interactive art at numerous institutions, including Dartmouth College, New York University, Bard College, and Fordham University. His website is [https://sharangbiswas.myportfolio.com](https://sharangbiswas.myportfolio.com), and you can find him on *Twitter* at [@SharangBiswas](http://twitter.com/SharangBiswas), and [itch.io](http://itch.io) at [https://astrolingus.itch.io/](https://astrolingus.itch.io/).

**Nathan Blades** is an androgynous android game show host from the future who works in broadcasting when not rolling dice. They produce a mixed-system actual play podcast called *The Talent Agency*, and have several written RPG works to their name, including *The Queer Cyberpunk’s Guide to Tabletop RPGs*.

**Ben Chong**, also known as Flowers, is a nonbinary, bi Malaysian-Chinese tabletop game designer making games about intimacy, relationships, tough conversations, and everyday magic. Their works are inspired by romance, surrealism, and horror with a Southeast Asian bent. Ben imagines deep, emotional worlds to touch the hearts of players. Under the name Swords and Flowers, Ben self-publishes; runs workshops, mentorship programs, and community events; and collaborates on international projects. They currently lecture for game design in UOW Malaysia KDU’s Bachelor of Game Development (Hons).

**Jay Dragon** is a lyrical and narrative game designer in the Hudson Valley who writes games about communities, queerness, monstrosity, found family, and hope. Jay can often be found sitting by the river, exploring the woods, and teaching kids about flower crowns.

**Jenn Ellis** and **Keith Baker** pursue their love of bringing people together through story-telling through their company Twogether Studios, with games ranging from the light-hearted *Action Cats!* to the RPG *Phoenix: Dawn Comman*d. Their latest game, *The Adventure Zone: Bureau of Balance*, lets any group of players follow in the footsteps of the McElroy family to create a fun fantasy adventure. Outside of Twogether Studios, Keith is also known for creating the storytelling card game Gloom and the Eberron Campaign Setting for *Dungeons & Dragons*.

Armed with a very niche college degree and a handful of semi-notable playwriting credits, **Alex Flanigan** entered the world of RPG writing the same way she entered most other pursuits: underprepared, overwhelmed, and highly caffeinated. She lives in Virginia—the boring one without all the best mountains—with one fiancé and one extremely good dog, and can be found every week on *A Horror Borealis* and *The Cryptid Keeper* wherever it is that you get your podcasts.

**Shanna Germain** is a writer, editor, and game designer, as well as the co-owner of Monte Cook Games. Her work includes *No Thank You, Evil!*, *Numenera*, *Invisible Sun*, *Predation*, *The Poison Eater*, *Tomorrow’s Bones*, and the forthcoming fairy tale game *We Are All Mad Here*. Visit her online at [ShannaGermain.com](http://ShannaGermain.com).

**Anil Godigamuwe** is a corporate wordslinger and cat shepherd (aka Communications Officer & Community Manager) for Rusty Quill, an independent entertainment company and podcast network. A long-time TRPG player who started with AD&D, he’s now collaborating on game design and working on his own game hacks. He’s also a variety streamer and host on the Rusty Quill Twitch channel. As a poet, Anil has performed at various conventions and Vogon slams. His work has been included in *Poems for the Queer Revolution* (2015) and the role-playing game *Lovecraftesque* (2016). You can find him managing the various Rusty Quill online presences ([@therustyquill](http://twitter.com/therustyquill)) or on his own *Twitter* ([@godigumdrop](http://twitter.com/godigumdrop)).

**Aly Grauer** is an author, actor, and podcaster based in Orlando, FL. She is a performer in theme parks and has published several short stories and at least one novel. She also teaches dialect lessons for theater and gaming. Her first game, *Sidewalkia!*, was a winner of the 200 Word RPG Challenge in 2018.

**James Mendez Hodes** is a martial artist, cultural consultant, writer, and editor based in the greater New York metropolitan area on traditional Lenape land. You might know him from his blog about cultural issues in nerddom, his award-winning RPG writing on projects like *7th Sea* and *Scion*, or his rap translation of Homer’s *Iliad*. He desperately wants to fight each and every one of you. Find him on the Internet at [JamesMendezHodes.com](http://JamesMendezHodes.com) and on *Twitter* at [@LulaVampiro](http://twitter.com/LulaVampiro).

**Grant Howitt** writes short games about criminal animals and long games about revolutionary elves. He is one-third of Rowan, Rook and Decard, one of the most successful RPG design studios in the United Kingdom.

**Levon Jihanian** (he/him) is best known in the game design world for designing *Fuck! It’s Dracula* and *Streets of Passion*. He created illustrations for *Firefly Role-Playing Game*, *The Forest Hymn and Picnic*, and *Under Hollow Hills*. His primary professional career is in the field of animation, and he’s worked on such shows as *Wander Over Yonder*, *Tig N’ Seek*, and the Emmy Award–winning *Over the Garden Wall*.

**Jonaya Kemper** is an activist, educator, games theorist, and designer who believes in the power of play as liberation. In addition to this, she works across the game design spectrum to create innovative, engaging gameplay and narrative, as well as consultation for more equitable gamespaces. Her efforts can be seen in analogue games, immersive experiences, and digital work, where she has collaborated with such companies as John Wick Presents 7th Sea, Renegade Game Studios, Participation Design Agency, and Remedy Entertainment. She is a proud Fairy Gothmother, and wrote *Tales from the Corner Coven* for all the bodegas and cats in New York City, New Jersey, and beyond.

**Daniel H. Kwan** is a Toronto-based podcaster, designer, cultural consultant, and educator. He hosts the ENnie Award–nominated *Asians Represent!* podcast and has written for publishers like Wizards of the Coast and Darker Hue Studios. Daniel is also one of the cofounders of Level Up Gaming, an organization that helps adults with autism and other disabilities develop their social skills through tabletop gaming experiences.

**Brandon Leon-Gambetta** is a Peruvian-American independent game designer and content creator best known for *Pasión de las Pasiones*, a tabletop game that emulates telenovelas. He is a host on the game design podcast *Stop, Hack, and Roll*, and a GM for superhero actual play podcast *Protean City Comics*. His previous work in the industry includes scenarios for *Mission: Accomplished!*, *The Maze*, and *Quietus*. He can be found on *Twitter* at [@bleongambetta](http://twitter.com/bleongambetta) or on his website [BrandonLeonGambetta.com](http://BrandonLeonGambetta.com).

**Angus E. Macpherson** (*Twitter*: [@ThePrimeAngus](http://twitter.com/ThePrimeAngus)) is a Toronto-based author, editor, role-playing game designer, and student at Victoria College at the University of Toronto. He is currently pursuing creative writing studies at the highest level available, under the tutelage of novelist David Gilmour (winner of the 2005 Governor General’s Award for English-language fiction) and Giller Prize–winning novelist John Bemrose.

**Jenn Martin** is a game designer, maker, and project manager. Her most recent game, *The Goose of Grillner Grove*, is about an otherwise peaceful neighborhood being menaced by a goose. She is the production manager for Bully Pulpit Games and LARP coordinator for Big Bad Con, a nonprofit tabletop gaming convention in California.

**Ash McAllan** is a gameplay and narrative designer working in digital, tabletop, and role-playing games. Her work channels a punk, queer, feminist outlook through a lens of system-first play design and an irreverence toward traditional narrative structures.

**Ben Meredith** is a systems and narrative designer for both video games and (obviously) tabletop RPGs. He’s also a voice actor, performing in the Rusty Quill podcasts *The Magnus Archives*, *Rusty Quill Gaming*, and *Stellar Firma*. You can find him on *Twitter* at [@benrlmeredith](http://twitter.com/benrlmeredith).

**Drew Mierzejewski** is a professional actor and creative currently living in Orlando, FL. He is also a writer with credits in *Shadowrun* and was a runner-up in the 200 Word RPG Challenge. When not writing or acting or dreaming big dreams, Drew is spending time with his wife, Alyson, and spoiling their two cats, Felicia and Wedge.

**Jason Morningstar** is an award-winning game and experience designer responsible for role-playing games like *Fiasco* and *Night Witches*, as well as playful foolishness for companies like Google and Kaiser Permanente. His work can be found at Bully Pulpit Games ([www.bullypulpitgames.com](http://www.bullypulpitgames.com)).

**Quinn Murphy** is a father, game designer, and engineer who enjoys using both halves of his brain. He has been hacking, designing, and discussing RPGs since he was eleven and believes imagination is for everyone.

**Brandon O’Brien** is a writer, performance poet, teaching artist, and game designer from Trinidad and Tobago. Their work has been shortlisted for the 2014 Alice Yard Prize for Art Writing and the 2014 and 2015 Small Axe Literary Competitions. They have been published in *Uncanny Magazine*, *Strange Horizons*, *Reckoning*, *Fireside Magazine*, and *New Worlds, Old Ways: Speculative Tales from the Caribbean*, among others. They are also a teaching artist with The 2 Cents Movement and the poetry editor of *FIYAH: Literary Magazine of Black Speculative Fiction*. Brandon can be found on *Facebook*, *Twitter*, and *Instagram* at [@therisingtithes](http://instagram.com/therisingtithes).

**Takuma Okada** (they/she) is a Japanese game designer, writer, and musician. She loves beautiful places and strange objects and tries to make them easy to find in her design. Their games, including *Alone Among the Stars* and *Stewpot: Tales from a Fantasy Tavern*, can be found at [noroadhome.itch.io](http://noroadhome.itch.io). She also helped run the *Emotional Mecha Jam* on [itch.io](http://itch.io) in 2019. They were a part of the *Beam Saber* playtest actual play podcast *The Cenotaph*, and have guested on several other shows, including the *Game Closet* and *Brain Trust* podcasts. Their current projects include a zine of three *Alone* games, as well as *The Black Box* actual play podcast, a dramaturgically incisive actual play.

**Nell Raban** is a software developer, independent game designer, and freelance writer. In 2019 she released *Home Again*, a role-playing game of magical realism and cultural reclamation. Her RPG writing has appeared in *ORUN* by New Agenda Publishing, the *MYTHIC D6: Adventure Anthology* by Khepera Publishing, *Quietus* by Oli Jeffery, and *The Maze* by Axo Stories. She tweets as [@nell_do_well](http://twitter.com/nell_do_well).

**Alex Roberts** is a writer and game maker whose work is defined by emotional complexity and mechanical elegance. Her cooperative storytelling games *Star Crossed* and *For the Queen* have won awards, introduced countless new players to role-playing, and been published in multiple languages around the world. She hosted the acclaimed interview podcast *BackStory*, has been a featured guest at gaming events like Gen Con, Big Bad Con, and Ropecon, and has contributed to games like *Dialect*, *Misspent Youth*, and the #Feminism collection. She loves long conversations, dandelion tea, and redesigning broken old board games she finds in the trash. Keep up with her at [HelloAlexRoberts.com](http://HelloAlexRoberts.com).

**Kienna** **Shaw** is a queer Chinese-Canadian designer, streamer, community organizer, academic, and advocate. She has written many games (both self-published on [itch.io](http://itch.io) and for indie game publishers) with a focus on relationships, self-reflection, emotions, and what it means to be human. Kienna is also the cocreator of the TTRPG Safety Toolkit, and focuses on doing community work centered around making the TTRPG community and industry safer, more inclusive, and well informed.

**Brie Beau Sheldon** (they/he) is a queer gender-fluid nonbinary masculine game designer, journalist, and photographer who loves leadership, community, and the supernatural. They are known for their work with Daedalum AP and projects such as *Turn*, *Let Me Take a Selfie*, *Of the Woods: Lonely Games of Imagination*, *Firefly RPG: Smuggler’s Guide to the Rim*, *Behind the Masc*, and the safety toolbox Script Change. Beau is located in the Pittsburgh, PA, area. They talk about their work on their blog *Thoughty*, which also features their hundreds of interviews with other designers and articles on game theory. They release those posts and smaller games via their *Patreon*, and publish games on their [itch.io](http://itch.io) site, *DriveThruRPG*, and Indie Press Revolution.

**Jeeyon Shim** makes games that strive to embody the natural world. Her games connect readers and players to the land around them, to their communities, to their senses, and to themselves. Her work has been featured with Bully Pulpit Games, Big Bad Con, Heart of the Deernicorn, and the SFWA Nebula Awards Reading List. When Jeeyon is not writing and designing games, she works as a naturalist and outdoor educator. She loves dirt.

**Jordan Shiveley** is a writer and designer of weird fictions and games. He also is the cohost of the *Caring Into the Void* podcast and writes the Dread Singles ([@hottestsingles](http://twitter.com/hottestsingles)) *Twitter* account. His website is [JordanShiveley.com](http://JordanShiveley.com).

**Sasha Sienna** is a writer, game designer, and professional nerd from London. They create storytelling games with Jonathan Sims through MacGuffin & Co., where they hold the official title of “basically in charge.” Sasha’s a sucker for compelling characters, playing with genre, and strong narrative structure. They especially love investigative and collaborative games. In addition to that they perform as Georgie on *The Magnus Achieves* and review Jane Austen adaptations on the *Jane Austentations* podcast. You can find them on *Twitter at* [@SiennaSasha](http://twitter.com/SiennaSasha).

**Laura Simpson** is a user experience designer by day and a game designer at night. She lives and works on indie games in Queens, NY, with her partner, Dev Purkayastha, as Sweet Potato Press. She has also designed the game *Companions’ Tale*.

**Johnathan Sims** is a writer, performer, game designer, and, in a decidedly predictable twist, was a ghost all along. In addition to writing and narrating *The Magnus Archives* for The Rusty Quill, he performs with space-pirate myth-singers *The Mechanisms* and authored the horror novel *Thirteen Storeys*. You can find more of his work at [Jonathan-Sims.com](http://Jonathan-Sims.com) or follow him on *Twitter* at [@jonnywaistcoat](http://twitter.com/jonnywaistcoat).

**Adira Slattery** is a game designer, podcaster, and zinester. She lives in Chicagoland with her spouse and two amazing cats. Always up for a new experience or a weird mechanic, Adira loves to exist in the experimental edges of tabletop and LARP. She makes games that explore intimacy, optimism, futility, and both queer and Jewish themes. Adira is also a member of the lyric games movement, focusing on emotional experiences and poetic expression through her design. Her self-published zines and games can be downloaded from [adira.itch.io](http://adira.itch.io).

**Pidj Sorensen** is an avid game master, writer, and player of role-playing games, having begun in high school. Many TTRPGs (tabletop role-playing games) feature very narrow concepts—a medieval European fantasy with violent yet somehow noble adventurers slaying “monsters,” for example. While those games can be fun, they’re pervasive. Pidj tries to provide alternate frameworks that allow for different narratives: nonviolence, a centering of indigenous values, games that are inclusive and thoughtful in their fun. In designing player-first mechanics, they draw on their psychology and computer science background. Pidj has a lot of hope for the future of TTRPGs—it is such an exciting time to design and play!

**Soup** (they/them) is a nonbinary Korean-American indie tabletop game designer who has been self-publishing since 2018. They started designing for a course on experimental tabletop game design in university and never really stopped. They mostly sit on a hoard of near-finished games waiting for formatting like some kind of esoteric game-developing hermit growing mushrooms deep in the forest. Soup’s design is largely influenced by the unhealthy amount of anime that they watch.
