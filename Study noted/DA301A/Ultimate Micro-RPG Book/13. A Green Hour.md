# A Green Hour

The Dying Land

By JEEYON SHIM

**Number of players:** *2*

**Playing time:** *1–2 hours*

**Complexity:** 2

**You’ll need:** *Paper, pens, 2d6 per player.*

**Goal:** *To simultaneously explore the intimacy of humanity and the vastness of being a god.*

**Tags:** *Genre: Fantasy | Tone: Serious | Format: GMless | Content: Intimacy, sex, cultural pressure*

In *A Green Hour*, you play the Scions of two ancient lineages. The land is dying. According to the temple, your arranged marriage will determine the fate of the land and the survival of the old gods—by uniting the two of you into a new god.

You stand before each other in the sacred bower devoted to your union. Before dawn, you will create the mantles of your new existence. This is the last time you will converse as human beings, with human worries and human hearts.

Everyone awaits your return.

## SETUP

Read through the entire text together before you begin play.

Assume that as people brought up in the traditions of the temple, both your characters consented wholeheartedly to the arrangement of your marriage and its duties. If that is a problem, this is probably not the game for you.

### WHO IS YOUR SCION?

As a Scion of the last land stewards, you take on the mantle of care for an aspect of the natural world. Start with these stats:

1. **Honorable:** Your Honorable starts at 3. Roll both your d6s for Honor to comfort your betrothed, fulfill your duties, and act on behalf of the temple. If both of your d6s are higher than 3, write the higher number here as your Honor stat. If either of your d6s are 3 or lower, take a 3.
2. **Impious:** Your Impious starts at 3. Roll Impious to reject the old gods, lash out emotionally, and betray the land. If both of your d6s are lower than 3, write the lower number here as your Impious stat. If either of your d6s are 3 or higher, take a 3.

### NAME YOUR SCION

You have a birth name, but the moment you were given to the temple it was burnt as an offering, and the ashes vanished at dawn.

Your temple name is: _____________

If you are stuck for ideas, try one of the following as a springboard: Acanthus, Valerian, Madling, Stag, Xilis, Ichor, Other.

### CHOOSE AN OLD GOD

Gods are defined by what they have domain over. The tide. Calm air. Wildfires. New growth. Sunlight. Rain.

Together, name your god.

### CHOOSE A TEMPTATION

There is something that beckons you away from your fate. What is it?

### CHOOSE A TENSION

Your family is at odds with the family of your betrothed. Why is there bad blood? How will your marriage heal it?

### ROLL 1D6 FOR YOUR RITUAL MANTLE

On the night of your wedding, you create a mantle that will become part of your new godhood. When you wear it, you are able to invoke its domain. Each player will roll for their own mantle.

- **1** **Blood Rites:** Don the blood of a sacrifice like a shimmering, wet robe.
- **2** **Sea’s Embrace:** Flood your bower with a warm tide, glowing with bioluminescence.
- **3** **Verdant Bed:** Sow your garden with living, tender young plants that sprout from your body.
- **4** **Singing Season:** Weave together the foxes crying, riotous birdsong, the wolves’ keening chorus in an improbable, perfect harmony.
- **5** **Sweet Waters:** Stand tall under the torrent of summer rain that falls only over the bower, soaking through.
- **6** **Root Crown:** Roots erupt from the leaf litter, shining bone-white, and wind around you and your betrothed.

## GAMEPLAY

There are **Three Parts** to *A Green Hour*.

1. **1**. Part One is **Alone Together**. Your last conversation as humans, before your union and transformation.
2. **2**. Part Two of the game is **A New God**. Your wedding altar is the loom on which you weave together your ritual mantles, to create a single, powerful new land god.
3. **3**. Part Three ends the game with **The Path Before You**, which takes place one hundred years from the events of A New God. Did you return to the temple and assume your sworn duties to the land? Or did you run?

At the end of each part, you will each roll both 2d6 twice. For the first roll, add the higher result to your Honorable stat. For the second roll, add the higher result to your Impious stat.

If your score exceeds 8 for Honorable, you are granted a boon, and something unexpected and beautiful will spring forth as the child of your union, and the land will heal. Together, describe your boon, the aspects of both of you that it embodies, and how it is essential to the new way of things.

If your score exceeds 8 for Impious, you have the option to deny your fate. To do so will free you, and you both will remain human, but your freedom will doom the land. Describe whether you run as fugitives together, or whether you part ways.

### PART ONE: ALONE TOGETHER

Answer the following prompts together out of character, then act out a conversation between the two of you.

**Family:**

1. **1**. My family stewards _______. I am responsible for _______.
2. **2**. Twining our two domains together will strengthen the land by _______.

**Meeting:** This is the first time you’ve laid eyes on your betrothed.

1. **1**. What strikes you as beautiful? Choose one to two:
    - Their hands
    - Their eyes
    - Their bearing
    - Their voice
2. **2**. What sparks a feeling of tenderness toward them? Choose one to two:
    - Their hands
    - Their eyes
    - Their bearing
    - Their voice

#### THE LAST HOUR

Your betrothed is the last person you will ever speak to as a human. Role-play the conversation, touching on all the prompts you answered in Family and Meeting.

Roll your d6s.

What does it look like if you receive either? Play it out.

### PART TWO: A NEW GOD

Your Scions now metamorphose into their new, united form as a land god. The ritual has four parts, named after the seasons. Answer the prompts:

**Autumn:** What parts of your humanity die?

**Winter:** What parts of your humanity do you carry into your new existence?

**Spring:** What does your new body look like, and what can it do?

**Summer:** What parts of the land do you reign over, and how does it celebrate your rebirth?

Roll to see if you can get a Boon or Deny. What does it look like if you receive either? Play it out.

## ENDING THE GAME

If You Returned to the Land As a God:

- Describe the processional that sprang from the land as you returned to the temple.
- One hundred years have passed. You are the last surviving land god. What are your duties? What are your chores? What does it feel like to live as the last of your kind?

If You Denied Your Stewardship:

- Describe the disasters that befell the land in your wake.

### PART THREE: THE PATH BEFORE YOU

One hundred years have passed. If you stayed human because you denied your duties after Part One, you’ve been cursed to live until the land itself dies. If you transformed but ran before returning to the temple, you are a fugitive god. What does your life look like now? How has the land recovered from your absence?
