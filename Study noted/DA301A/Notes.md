# Games as metaphor
Poetry puts words to emotion; video games puts action to emotion.

Doris C. Rusch:
1. Fictional alignment. What you feel about the elements in the game. Should be congruent with mechanics.
2. Procedurality. Can mirror RL systems.
3. Experiental metaphor. The experience itself is the metaphor.

Alex Mitchell, Game Mechanics as Narrative Mode:
* Interface metaphor.
	* Concrete objects are still abstracted in the game.
* Experiential metaphor.
	* The items, quests, stuff, conveying the abstract concept.
* Poetic gameplay
	* By undermining expectations, you draw attention to game mechanics and thereby encourage reflection.
