## Lecture 2: Discreet mathematics
Factorised: $a(b+c)$, expanded: $ab+ac$.
$-(b+c)=b-c$ but $-(b-c)=(-b)+c$. If the parenthesis is positive, don't flip the sign.

**Expand:**
$$x^2-2y-2x(x-(y-x))$$ 

**Conjugation rule**
$$(a+b)×(a-b)=a^2-ab+ba-b^2=a^2-b^2$$

**Factorizing**
$$8ab-24a^2=8a(b-3a)$$
### Division
**Lowest common denominator:**
$$
\frac{12}{150}=\frac{3×4}{15×10}=\frac{3×2×2}{3×5×x×5}=\frac{2}{5×5}=\frac{2}{25}
$$
$$
\frac{4}{14}-\frac{1}{6}=\frac{2×4}{2×15}-\frac{5×1}{5×6}=\frac{8}{30}-\frac{5}{30}=\frac{8-5}{30}=\frac{3}{30}=\frac{3}{3×10}=\frac{1}{10}
$$

**Multiply:**
$$\frac{a}{b}×\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ac}{bd}$$

**Inverse:**
The fraction that can be added to make the result 1. $\frac{a}{b}$ → $\frac{b}{a}$.

**Simplify example:**
$$
\frac{b}{a^2+ab}+\frac{1}{a+b}=\frac{b}{a^2+ab}+\frac{a×1}{a(a+b)}=\frac{b}{a(a+b)}+\frac{a}{a(a+b)}=\frac{a+b}{a(a+b)}=\frac{1}{a}
$$

### Exponents
Squared, cubed... hypercubed?
$0^0=NaN$.
$5^{-1}=\frac{1}{5}$ i.e. $a^{-1}=\frac{1}{a^n}$.

**Rules (when $a\neq 0$)**
* $a^0=1$
* $a^{-k}=\frac{a}{a^k}$
* $a^m+a^n=a^{m+n}$
* $(a^m)^n=a^{m×n}$
* $\frac{a^n}{a^m}=a^{a-m}$
* $(ab)^n=a^n×b^n$
* $\left( \frac{a}{b} \right)^n=\frac{a^n}{b^n}$
* $a^{1/n}=\sqrt[n]{a}$

**Roots**
* $\sqrt{xy}=\sqrt{x}×\sqrt{y}$
* $\sqrt(x+y)\neq\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y}$

	* $\cup$ combines everything
